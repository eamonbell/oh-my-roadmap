# /milestone:close

Close the active milestone only after inspecting the plan, notes, changed code, relevant documentation, and verification evidence. Use the built-in `ask` tool for any unresolved deferral, risk disposition, acceptance, verification, cleanup, or approval decision. Structured evidence must map verification commands and acceptance criteria to `passed` or approved `deferred` results. Use `roadmap_engineer_transition` with `record_closeout` before `complete_milestone`. Append unresolved risks with dispositions and approved deferrals.
